How it works

  1. A call comes in. A customer calls a participating business.
  2. If it's missed, a text goes out. If the call is missed, an automated SMS can be sent to the customer to continue the conversation.
  3. The automated assistant can continue the SMS conversation until the business is available to respond. It answers common questions and helps schedule follow-up, all over text, until a human takes over.

Our AI receptionist solutions are intended for businesses that rely on incoming customer phone calls, including plumbing, HVAC, electrical, locksmith, appliance repair and similar home-service businesses.

These solutions help businesses continue customer conversations through SMS when calls cannot be answered immediately.
