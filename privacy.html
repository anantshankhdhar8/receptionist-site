Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ANANT SHANKHDHAR ("we", "our", or "us") provides AI receptionist and automated SMS communication services for small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you interact with our website or business phone number.

Information we collect

When you contact us through our website, submit our request form, call our business phone number, or send us a text message, we may collect your name, business name, phone number, email address, and the content of your communication.

How we use your information

We use this information only to respond to your enquiries, provide requested information about our services, operate our automated SMS communication services, and maintain records of conversations initiated by you.

Sharing of personal information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent — that information is not shared with any third parties, under any circumstance.

Data retention

We retain conversation records only as long as needed to provide the service and to demonstrate consent and opt-out status if requested by a carrier or regulator.

Opt-out

You may stop receiving text messages from us at any time by replying STOP to any message. You'll receive one final confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent to that number unless you contact us again. Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to SMS Communications

By submitting your information through our website request form, calling our business phone number, or otherwise contacting us regarding our services, you consent to receive SMS communications related to your enquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.
